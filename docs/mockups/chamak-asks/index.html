    Options: 📚 Silly & funny stories (audio) · 🎮 Mini-games & puzzles (spelling bee) · ⚔️ Challenges with friends (share 5 Qs) · 🛠️ Making my own quests.
  • Poll 2: "Would you like to make a quest for other kids?"
    Options: 🤩 Yes, I already have ideas! · 🤔 Maybe, if it's easy · 🎮 I just want to play them!
  • Poll 3: "What would you do if a friend challenged you?"
    Options: ⚡ Play right away! · 🛡️ Practise first to beat them · 😊 Laugh & share my score.

🛡️ Child Safety & Voice v2 Architecture

  • No Open Chat / Social Exposure: Children can only pick pre-vetted options or type into a private supervised note sent strictly to the admin.
  • Supervised Text: Text box only appears for verified supervised accounts whose parent has consented to typing.
  • Voice (🎤 Say it) is v2: Child voice recordings constitute sensitive biometric/personal data under COPPA and India's DPDP Act. v2 requires native audio recording library, explicit parent permission in the consent sheet, and a privacy policy update.

💾 Firestore Data Model

  • polls/{pollId}: Poll question, options, emoji icons, reward stars, active dates. Allows updating polls without an app release.
  • pollResponses/{responseId}: pollId, userId, selectedOptionIndex, typedText, createdAt.
  • users/{uid}/answeredPolls/{pollId}: Tracks completion so the sticky card automatically disappears once answered.
