## Pronoun Challenge

### Personal & possessive

1. Fill in the blank: 'Riya and ___ went to the fair.'
   - ✓ **I**
   - ✗ me
   - ✗ myself
   - ✗ mine
   - _Why:_ Take away 'Riya and': we say 'I went', not 'me went'. So: 'Riya and I went to the fair.'

2. Fill in the blank: 'This book is ___. My name is written on it.'
   - ✓ **mine**
   - ✗ my
   - ✗ me
   - ✗ I
   - _Why:_ 'mine' stands alone and means 'belonging to me'. 'my' always needs a noun after it: my book.

3. Fill in the blank: 'The teacher praised ___ for our hard work.'
   - ✓ **us**
   - ✗ we
   - ✗ our
   - ✗ ours
   - _Why:_ After the verb 'praised' we need an object pronoun: us.

4. Which word is a possessive pronoun? 'The red kite is hers.'
   - ✓ **hers**
   - ✗ red
   - ✗ kite
   - ✗ is
   - _Why:_ 'hers' tells us whom the kite belongs to, and it stands in place of 'her kite'.

5. Fill in the blank: 'The dog wagged ___ tail.'
   - ✓ **its**
   - ✗ it's
   - ✗ it
   - ✗ their
   - _Why:_ 'its' (no apostrophe) means 'belonging to it'. 'it's' means 'it is'.

### Reflexive, demonstrative & indefinite

1. Fill in the blank: 'He hurt ___ while playing cricket.'
   - ✓ **himself**
   - ✗ hisself
   - ✗ him
   - ✗ themself
   - _Why:_ The person who got hurt is the same person who did it, so we use the reflexive pronoun 'himself'. 'hisself' is not a word.

2. Fill in the blank: 'We cleaned the room by ___.'
   - ✓ **ourselves**
   - ✗ ourself
   - ✗ us
   - ✗ ourselfs
   - _Why:_ For 'we', the reflexive pronoun is 'ourselves' (plural: -selves).

3. Fill in the blank: '___ are my shoes, and those are yours.'
   - ✓ **These**
   - ✗ This
   - ✗ That
   - ✗ Them
   - _Why:_ 'shoes' is plural and near me, so we use 'these'. 'This' is for one thing.

4. Fill in the blank: '___ knocked on the door, but when I opened it, nobody was there.'
   - ✓ **Someone**
   - ✗ Anyone
   - ✗ Everyone
   - ✗ Nobody
   - _Why:_ One unknown person knocked, so we say 'someone'. 'Anyone', 'everyone' and 'nobody' don't fit this sentence.

5. Which sentence uses a reflexive pronoun correctly?
   - ✓ **The cat licked itself.**
   - ✗ She gave the book to myself.
   - ✗ They enjoyed themself at the party.
   - ✗ Myself went to school.
   - _Why:_ A reflexive pronoun points back to the subject: the cat licked the cat, so 'itself'.

### Relative & question words

1. Fill in the blank: 'The boy ___ won the race is my cousin.'
   - ✓ **who**
   - ✗ which
   - ✗ whom
   - ✗ whose
   - _Why:_ We use 'who' for people when they do the action (the boy won).

2. Fill in the blank: 'This is the pen ___ I bought yesterday.'
   - ✓ **which**
   - ✗ who
   - ✗ whose
   - ✗ whom
   - _Why:_ We use 'which' (or 'that') for things. 'who' and 'whom' are for people.

3. Fill in the blank: '___ bag is this?'
   - ✓ **Whose**
   - ✗ Who's
   - ✗ Who
   - ✗ Whom
   - _Why:_ 'Whose' asks who something belongs to. 'Who's' means 'who is'.

4. Fill in the blank: '___ of these two dresses do you like?'
   - ✓ **Which**
   - ✗ What
   - ✗ Who
   - ✗ Whose
   - _Why:_ We use 'which' to choose from a small set of things: which of these two.

5. Fill in the blank: 'I met a girl ___ father is a pilot.'
   - ✓ **whose**
   - ✗ who
   - ✗ which
   - ✗ whom
   - _Why:_ 'whose' shows that something belongs to someone: the girl's father.

## Adjective Challenge

### Finding adjectives

1. Find the adjective: 'The playful puppy chased the ball.'
   - ✓ **playful**
   - ✗ puppy
   - ✗ chased
   - ✗ ball
   - _Why:_ 'playful' describes the puppy (a noun), so it is an adjective.

2. Find the adjective: 'We saw a huge elephant at the zoo.'
   - ✓ **huge**
   - ✗ saw
   - ✗ elephant
   - ✗ zoo
   - _Why:_ 'huge' tells us what the elephant is like.

3. Which word describes the noun? 'Grandma wore a beautiful saree.'
   - ✓ **beautiful**
   - ✗ Grandma
   - ✗ wore
   - ✗ saree
   - _Why:_ 'beautiful' describes the saree.

4. In 'The sky looks cloudy today', which word is an adjective?
   - ✓ **cloudy**
   - ✗ sky
   - ✗ looks
   - ✗ today
   - _Why:_ 'cloudy' describes the sky. 'looks' links the sky to how it is.

5. Which sentence has TWO adjectives?
   - ✓ **A tall, thin man opened the door.**
   - ✗ A man opened the door.
   - ✗ The man quickly opened the door.
   - ✗ The man opened the old door.
   - _Why:_ 'tall' and 'thin' both describe the man. 'quickly' is an adverb.

### Kinds of adjectives

1. What kind of adjective is 'five' in 'She has five pencils'?
   - ✓ **Adjective of number**
   - ✗ Adjective of quality
   - ✗ Demonstrative adjective
   - ✗ Adjective of quantity
   - _Why:_ 'five' tells HOW MANY pencils, so it is an adjective of number.

2. What kind of adjective is 'some' in 'Please add some sugar'?
   - ✓ **Adjective of quantity**
   - ✗ Adjective of number
   - ✗ Adjective of quality
   - ✗ Demonstrative adjective
   - _Why:_ Sugar can't be counted, so 'some' tells HOW MUCH: an adjective of quantity.

3. Find the demonstrative adjective: 'Those mangoes are sweet.'
   - ✓ **Those**
   - ✗ mangoes
   - ✗ are
   - ✗ sweet
   - _Why:_ 'Those' points out which mangoes. 'sweet' is an adjective of quality.

4. Which word is an adjective of quality?
   - ✓ **brave**
   - ✗ three
   - ✗ much
   - ✗ these
   - _Why:_ 'brave' tells what kind (quality). 'three' is number, 'much' is quantity, 'these' is demonstrative.

5. Fill in the blank: 'There isn't ___ milk in the jug.'
   - ✓ **much**
   - ✗ many
   - ✗ a few
   - ✗ several
   - _Why:_ Milk can't be counted, so we use 'much'. 'many', 'a few' and 'several' are for things we can count.

### Degrees of comparison

1. Fill in the blank: 'Rohan is ___ than his brother.'
   - ✓ **taller**
   - ✗ tall
   - ✗ tallest
   - ✗ more taller
   - _Why:_ Comparing two people → comparative: taller than. Never 'more taller'.

2. Fill in the blank: 'This is the ___ day of the year.'
   - ✓ **hottest**
   - ✗ hotter
   - ✗ hotest
   - ✗ most hot
   - _Why:_ Comparing with all days → superlative. hot → hottest (double the t).

3. Choose the correct sentence.
   - ✓ **This puzzle is more difficult than that one.**
   - ✗ This puzzle is difficulter than that one.
   - ✗ This puzzle is most difficult than that one.
   - ✗ This puzzle is more difficulter than that one.
   - _Why:_ Long adjectives use 'more' for the comparative: more difficult than.

4. What is the superlative of 'good'?
   - ✓ **best**
   - ✗ goodest
   - ✗ better
   - ✗ most good
   - _Why:_ good → better → best. It's a special (irregular) adjective.

5. Fill in the blank: 'Mount Everest is ___ mountain in the world.'
   - ✓ **the highest**
   - ✗ higher
   - ✗ the higher
   - ✗ highest
   - _Why:_ Comparing with all mountains → superlative, and superlatives take 'the': the highest.

## Adverb Challenge

### How, when, where, how often

1. Which word tells WHEN? 'We will visit Grandpa tomorrow.'
   - ✓ **tomorrow**
   - ✗ visit
   - ✗ Grandpa
   - ✗ will
   - _Why:_ 'tomorrow' tells WHEN we will visit: an adverb of time.

2. Which word tells WHERE? 'The cat is hiding upstairs.'
   - ✓ **upstairs**
   - ✗ cat
   - ✗ hiding
   - ✗ is
   - _Why:_ 'upstairs' tells WHERE the cat is hiding: an adverb of place.

3. Which word tells HOW? 'She whispered softly to the baby.'
   - ✓ **softly**
   - ✗ whispered
   - ✗ baby
   - ✗ to
   - _Why:_ 'softly' tells HOW she whispered: an adverb of manner.

4. Which word tells HOW OFTEN? 'He rarely eats sweets.'
   - ✓ **rarely**
   - ✗ He
   - ✗ eats
   - ✗ sweets
   - _Why:_ 'rarely' tells HOW OFTEN he eats sweets: an adverb of frequency.

5. Choose the adverb of time.
   - ✓ **yesterday**
   - ✗ carefully
   - ✗ here
   - ✗ always
   - _Why:_ 'yesterday' tells WHEN. 'carefully' is manner, 'here' is place, 'always' is frequency.

### Adverb or adjective?

1. Choose the correct word: 'Riya sings ___.'
   - ✓ **beautifully**
   - ✗ beautiful
   - ✗ beauty
   - ✗ beautify
   - _Why:_ We need a word that tells HOW she sings (a verb), so the adverb 'beautifully'.

2. Choose the correct word: 'The soup smells ___.'
   - ✓ **delicious**
   - ✗ deliciously
   - ✗ deliciousness
   - ✗ delicacy
   - _Why:_ 'smells' is a linking verb here: it describes the soup, so we use the adjective 'delicious'.

3. In 'He is a fast runner', 'fast' is ___.
   - ✓ **an adjective**
   - ✗ an adverb
   - ✗ a verb
   - ✗ a noun
   - _Why:_ Here 'fast' describes the runner (a noun), so it is an adjective.

4. In 'He runs fast', 'fast' is ___.
   - ✓ **an adverb**
   - ✗ an adjective
   - ✗ a verb
   - ✗ a noun
   - _Why:_ Here 'fast' tells HOW he runs (a verb), so it is an adverb. Same word, different job!

5. Choose the correct sentence.
   - ✓ **She did well in the test.**
   - ✗ She did good in the test.
   - ✗ She did goodly in the test.
   - ✗ She did bestly in the test.
   - _Why:_ To tell HOW she did, we need the adverb 'well'. 'good' is an adjective.

### Making adverbs

1. Make an adverb from 'happy'.
   - ✓ **happily**
   - ✗ happyly
   - ✗ happly
   - ✗ happiness
   - _Why:_ Consonant + y: change y to i and add -ly. happy → happily.

2. Make an adverb from 'careful'.
   - ✓ **carefully**
   - ✗ carefuly
   - ✗ careful
   - ✗ carefulness
   - _Why:_ careful + ly = carefully (keep both l's).

3. Make an adverb from 'gentle'.
   - ✓ **gently**
   - ✗ gentlely
   - ✗ gentily
   - ✗ gentleness
   - _Why:_ Words ending in -le drop the e and add -y: gentle → gently.

4. What is the adverb that goes with the adjective 'good'?
   - ✓ **well**
   - ✗ goodily
   - ✗ better
   - ✗ goodness
   - _Why:_ good (adjective) → well (adverb): a good singer sings well.

5. Which word is NOT an adverb?
   - ✓ **friendly**
   - ✗ quickly
   - ✗ loudly
   - ✗ sadly
   - _Why:_ 'friendly' ends in -ly but describes a noun (a friendly dog), so it is an adjective.

## Preposition Challenge

### Place

1. Fill in the blank: 'My cousin lives ___ Mumbai.'
   - ✓ **in**
   - ✗ at
   - ✗ on
   - ✗ into
   - _Why:_ We use 'in' for cities and countries: in Mumbai, in India.

2. Fill in the blank: 'Meet me ___ the bus stop.'
   - ✓ **at**
   - ✗ in
   - ✗ on
   - ✗ into
   - _Why:_ We use 'at' for a particular point or spot: at the bus stop, at the door.

3. Fill in the blank: 'The fish are swimming ___ the pond.'
   - ✓ **in**
   - ✗ on
   - ✗ at
   - ✗ between
   - _Why:_ The fish are inside the water, so we use 'in'.

4. Fill in the blank: 'The picture is hanging ___ the wall.'
   - ✓ **on**
   - ✗ in
   - ✗ at
   - ✗ into
   - _Why:_ The picture touches the surface of the wall, so we use 'on'.

### Time

1. Fill in the blank: 'School starts ___ 8 o'clock.'
   - ✓ **at**
   - ✗ on
   - ✗ in
   - ✗ since
   - _Why:_ We use 'at' for clock times: at 8 o'clock, at noon.

2. Fill in the blank: 'My birthday is ___ Monday.'
   - ✓ **on**
   - ✗ in
   - ✗ at
   - ✗ since
   - _Why:_ We use 'on' for days and dates: on Monday, on 5th June.

3. Fill in the blank: 'We go to the hills ___ May.'
   - ✓ **in**
   - ✗ on
   - ✗ at
   - ✗ since
   - _Why:_ We use 'in' for months, years and seasons: in May, in 2025, in winter.

4. Fill in the blank: 'I have lived here ___ 2019.'
   - ✓ **since**
   - ✗ for
   - ✗ on
   - ✗ at
   - _Why:_ 'since' + a starting point in time (since 2019). 'for' + a length of time (for six years).

### Direction

1. Fill in the blank: 'Rahul walked ___ the room and sat down.'
   - ✓ **into**
   - ✗ on
   - ✗ at
   - ✗ of
   - _Why:_ 'into' shows movement from outside to inside.

2. Fill in the blank: 'The train goes ___ a long tunnel.'
   - ✓ **through**
   - ✗ across
   - ✗ on
   - ✗ at
   - _Why:_ 'through' means in at one end and out at the other, like a tunnel.

3. Fill in the blank: 'The children crossed carefully, walking ___ the road.'
   - ✓ **across**
   - ✗ through
   - ✗ in
   - ✗ of
   - _Why:_ 'across' means from one side to the other side of something flat, like a road.

### Fixed prepositions

1. Fill in the blank: 'She is afraid ___ spiders.'
   - ✓ **of**
   - ✗ from
   - ✗ with
   - ✗ by
   - _Why:_ We always say 'afraid of'. Some words go with fixed prepositions; learn them as pairs!

2. Fill in the blank: 'We are proud ___ our team.'
   - ✓ **of**
   - ✗ on
   - ✗ for
   - ✗ with
   - _Why:_ The fixed pair is 'proud of'.

3. Fill in the blank: 'He is good ___ drawing.'
   - ✓ **at**
   - ✗ in
   - ✗ on
   - ✗ with
   - _Why:_ The fixed pair is 'good at' (good at drawing, good at maths).

4. Fill in the blank: 'Please wait ___ me at the gate.'
   - ✓ **for**
   - ✗ to
   - ✗ at
   - ✗ with
   - _Why:_ The fixed pair is 'wait for' someone.

## Conjunction Challenge

### and, but, or, so

1. Fill in the blank: 'I wanted to play outside, ___ it was raining.'
   - ✓ **but**
   - ✗ and
   - ✗ so
   - ✗ or
   - _Why:_ The two ideas are opposite (wanted to play / it was raining), so we use 'but'.

2. Fill in the blank: 'Would you like tea ___ coffee?'
   - ✓ **or**
   - ✗ but
   - ✗ so
   - ✗ because
   - _Why:_ We use 'or' to give a choice.

3. Fill in the blank: 'Ravi was hungry, ___ he ate a sandwich.'
   - ✓ **so**
   - ✗ but
   - ✗ or
   - ✗ although
   - _Why:_ Being hungry is the reason, eating is the result, so we use 'so'.

4. Fill in the blank: 'Meera ___ her brother went to the market together.'
   - ✓ **and**
   - ✗ but
   - ✗ so
   - ✗ because
   - _Why:_ We use 'and' to join two people who did the same thing.

5. Which word is a conjunction? 'I like apples and bananas.'
   - ✓ **and**
   - ✗ like
   - ✗ apples
   - ✗ I
   - _Why:_ 'and' joins 'apples' and 'bananas', so it is a conjunction.

### because, although, unless, when

1. Fill in the blank: 'We stayed indoors ___ it was very hot.'
   - ✓ **because**
   - ✗ although
   - ✗ unless
   - ✗ or
   - _Why:_ The heat is the reason we stayed indoors, so we use 'because'.

2. Fill in the blank: '___ he was tired, he finished his homework.'
   - ✓ **Although**
   - ✗ Because
   - ✗ So
   - ✗ Unless
   - _Why:_ Being tired and still finishing are a surprise together, so we use 'although'.

3. Fill in the blank: 'You will miss the bus ___ you hurry.'
   - ✓ **unless**
   - ✗ if
   - ✗ because
   - ✗ and
   - _Why:_ 'unless' means 'if not': you will miss the bus if you do not hurry.

4. Fill in the blank: 'Call me ___ you reach home.'
   - ✓ **when**
   - ✗ although
   - ✗ but
   - ✗ or
   - _Why:_ 'when' tells the time something happens: call me at the time you reach home.

5. Which sentence uses 'because' correctly?
   - ✓ **She smiled because she won the prize.**
   - ✗ She won the prize because.
   - ✗ Because she smiled the prize.
   - ✗ She smiled because the prize.
   - _Why:_ After 'because' we need a full reason with a subject and a verb: she won the prize.

### Pairs (either…or, neither…nor)

1. Fill in the blank: '___ Asha nor Tina came to the party.'
   - ✓ **Neither**
   - ✗ Either
   - ✗ Both
   - ✗ Not only
   - _Why:_ 'nor' always pairs with 'neither': neither … nor.

2. Fill in the blank: 'You can have ___ the red one or the blue one.'
   - ✓ **either**
   - ✗ neither
   - ✗ both
   - ✗ not only
   - _Why:_ 'or' pairs with 'either': either … or.

3. Fill in the blank: '___ my mother and my father are doctors.'
   - ✓ **Both**
   - ✗ Either
   - ✗ Neither
   - ✗ Not only
   - _Why:_ 'and' pairs with 'both': both … and.

4. Fill in the blank: 'He is not only clever ___ hard-working.'
   - ✓ **but also**
   - ✗ and also
   - ✗ or
   - ✗ nor
   - _Why:_ 'not only' pairs with 'but also'.

5. Fill in the blank: 'I don't know ___ he will come or not.'
   - ✓ **whether**
   - ✗ either
   - ✗ neither
   - ✗ both
   - _Why:_ We use 'whether … or not' when there are two possibilities.
