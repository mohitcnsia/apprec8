# ISO (was NSO) Class 7: what the exam looks like

**Source:** the SOF/MTG Class 7 model test paper the owner shared (`docs/exam/papers/class7/iso/`, git-ignored, scanned
images), read 17 Nov 2026. Styles and level only: every question we write is our own. Same 50-question shape for Classes 5–8.

## Shape (60 minutes, 50 questions)
| Section | Questions | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| Logical reasoning | 1–10 | operator swap (`+` means `÷`…), letter coding, circular seating, Venn counting, paper folding/cutting, number–letter series, blood relations, letter matrix, word from numbered letters, dice faces |
| Science | 11–45 | the 12 chapters of the Class 7 book |
| Achievers | 46–50 | longer, multi-step: pendulum timing, passage with blanks P/Q/R/S, dichotomous flowchart, circuit test of materials |

## Why our Class 7 warm-ups are too easy
Our warm-ups ask one fact at a time ("Which tubes carry food?"). The paper almost never does. Most questions need two or
three facts combined, or reading a table, diagram or experiment. Recall alone gets a kid maybe a third of the marks.

## Question styles to copy (style only, invented examples)
1. **Several statements, pick the right set:** "Which statements are INCORRECT? I. … II. … III. … IV. …" → "I and III only".
   The commonest style. Wrong statements are near misses (reversed cause, wrong organ, swapped term).
2. **Table with mismatches:** a 4–5 row table (part → function, or aerobic vs anaerobic) → "Which rows are wrong?"
3. **Match the columns with codes:** (i)–(iii) against (a)–(c) → "(i)-(c), (ii)-(a), (iii)-(b)".
4. **Statement 1 / Statement 2 (assertion–reason):** both true and 2 explains 1 · both true, no link · 1 true, 2 false · both false.
5. **Venn diagram:** three overlapping groups (physical/chemical/fast change; breathes by skin/lungs/gills; alloys of Cu/Zn/Sn)
   → which example sits where. We can do this in text: "in both A and B but not C".
6. **Experiment set-ups:** what a test shows, or which control is missing (litmus on 4 solutions, lime water + KOH and
   germinating seeds, rusting in 3 bottles with oil / silica gel, two flasks cooling).
7. **Short calculations:** average speed from a table of times and odometer readings; average speed over two legs of a
   trip; pendulum (time from one end to the middle × 4 = one oscillation).
8. **Formulas and names:** slaked lime Ca(OH)₂, quick lime CaO, calamine ZnCO₃; lab apparatus; scientists.
9. **Passage with blanks P, Q, R, S:** a short paragraph (e.g. on anaerobic respiration in muscles) → which statement about
   P–S is wrong.
10. **Flowchart key:** yes/no boxes ("autotroph? → grows in nitrogen-poor soil?") → identify P, Q, R.
11. **Unscramble + match:** jumbled words for terms, then match them to definitions.

## What this means for the app
- Each Class 7 topic keeps its warm-up (recall) and gets an **exam-style practice** of about 10 questions in the styles
  above (text only: tables, statements and codes are written out; no book diagrams).
- Options can be codes ("I and III only"), which keeps them short (≤ 14 words) even for long questions.
- Reasoning (10 of 50 questions) needs its own practice: text-only types first, figures drawn by our own code later.
- Chapters of the Class 7 book (2025): The Ever-Evolving World of Science · Exploring Substances: Acidic, Basic and Neutral ·
  Electricity: Circuits and their Components · The World of Metals and Non-metals · Changes Around Us: Physical and
  Chemical · Adolescence · Heat Transfer in Nature · Measurement of Time and Motion · Life Processes in Animals ·
  Life Processes in Plants · Light: Shadows and Reflections · Earth, Moon and the Sun.
  Missing from our 13 Class 7 topics: World of Science, Metals and Non-metals, Adolescence, Earth, Moon and the Sun.
