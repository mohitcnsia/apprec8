# App map: everything in Apprec8, in one page (Claude, updated 2026-11-10)

For you and Seema: what each part is, who sees it, and where it is. To try it hands-on: **Me → Guides → Tour of Apprec8**.

### 🧒 What a child sees
- **Home**: **"From your teacher"** (assigned work) comes first, then quest tiles (English, Science, Word Power, Treasure Words) and the teacher's course tile (e.g. "Seema Ma'am · Class 10 English"). Only quests with the child's class topics show. ▶ continues where they left off. "Coming up" = reminders for tests.
- **Quest map**: one path of topics. Each topic = **Learn cards** (short lessons) → **practice** (questions with a "why" after every answer) → a **Boss** at the end of a unit opens the next unit. Locks = not yet. Only the child's class topics show.
- **Reading**: a short story read aloud, then questions.
- **"Why not this option?"**: after a wrong answer, why each wrong option is wrong.
- **My mistakes** (button at the bottom of a map): wrong answers come back until right twice.
- **Practice papers** (Home → Explore, Class 5): a full paper with a clock, marked at the end.
- **Check-up** (Home → Explore, Classes 9–10): 54 questions across the 18 Grammar Pro topics, 49 min → **Accuracy by topic**, weakest first; **Practise this** opens that topic. Check-up 2 has different questions. Offline: "No internet. Your answers are safe on this phone" and it sends them later.
- **Progress**: stars, streak 🔥, time, strong topics, one topic to practise. Leaderboard shows first names + class only.
- **Me**: Need help (helplines 1098 / 14416 / 112), reminders, messages, **Join a class** (needs a linked parent: otherwise "Ask your parent to add you"), link to a parent, Tell us, Guides, delete account.
- **Guides → Your first day**: 4 steps that teach the app by doing.

### 👪 What a parent sees
- **Me → My children**: add a child (name + class, the parent agrees once), or link a child who has their own login (6-number code). A linked parent = the consent a child needs to join a class.
- **Play as**: the child plays on the parent's phone with their own stars; optional play-time timer; the parent PIN brings the parent back.
- **Progress → family**: each child's week + "one thing to do at home". English and हिंदी.
- **Sunday summary**: one notification per child on Sunday evening (turn on in Progress).
- **Guides → For parents** (4 steps) and **Tour of Apprec8** (6 screens).

### 👩‍🏫 What a teacher sees
- **Join as a teacher** with the school admin's code → the admin approves.
- **Home**: **Needs you** (kids waiting → approve) at the top, then the class card (kids active, weakest topic).
- **Progress → classes**: share the class code, approve students (only kids with a linked parent can ask: "parent: linked account ✓"), assign a topic with a due date, see each student's week and weak topics, **Check-up by topic** (class Accuracy per topic + names under 60%).
- **Monday summary**: one notification per class.
- **Guides → Start your class** (5 steps).

### 🏫 School admin (you, and admins you add)
- **Me → Admin tools → Schools**: create a school, add classes, invite teachers (one-time code), approve teachers. On a class: **"Pilot: open all levels for these kids"** (one tap, tap again to close).
- **Guides → Set up a school** (5 steps). **Demo School**: fake school for Google's reviewer and tours (Admin tools → Reset).

### 🔑 Only you (owner)
- **Me → Decisions**: questions from Claude; tap Yes / No / More details / Later. A notification tells you when new ones arrive.
- **Me → Admin tools**: My notes (launch checklist, testing, store listing, app map, Play forms, Master the app, Seema's pilot), Dashboard (every kid's progress), seed buttons, Demo School.
- **Fun tab and old quizzes** (Space, dogs, animals): hidden from everyone else for now.

### 🔜 Coming (on Gemini's board)
- **Two-way cards on Home** (G89): we ask, they answer in one tap, we reply. Replaces "Tell us".
- **Weekly report** (G92): Sunday evening; each parent sees only their own child; the teacher taps Share to send a no-names summary to the class WhatsApp group (optional Sunday reminder). English or हिंदी.
- **Teacher removes a kid from the class** (G97; the kid keeps their account and progress).
- **Approval pushes** (G94): "Student waiting" / "Teacher waiting" notifications.

### 🛡️ Safety and privacy, everywhere
No ads, no chat, children tap only (no typing to others). Children learn right away; a parent or guardian says yes before their progress is saved to their name or they join a class or leaderboard ("learn first", built, in review). Delete an account from Me any time.
