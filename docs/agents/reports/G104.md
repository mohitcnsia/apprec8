## Findings & Discrepancies
1. **Quest ID Mapping (`english` vs `english-quest`):**
   - The bundled quest registry key in `data/quests/index.js` is `english-quest`.
   - When assigning topics, `questId` must be `english-quest` (not `english`) so the app's `useAssignedTopics` hook successfully resolves the node title and study content.
2. **Sandbox Content Seeding Requirement:**
   - In a fresh test project, `EnglishQuestMapScreen` relies on `quests/{questId}/nodes` in Firestore.
   - Without seeding, the map opens to a blank screen. Seeding script populated 267 nodes and study content in `apprec8-test`.
3. **Score Recording Timing in Quest Quizzes:**
   - In training mode, individual questions log answers in memory (`answerLogRef`).
   - The persistent score and attempt record (`questAttempts` document, `users/{uid}/questProgress/{questId}` document, and `recordQuizResult` callable) write upon quiz completion (`status === "finished"`) or when out of hearts (`status === "failed"`).
