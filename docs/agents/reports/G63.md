## 4. The 4 Mood States & Duolingo-Level Expressiveness

To satisfy Pratha's requirement that our mascots must be dynamic and expressive, every character is designed with distinct facial landmarks and body language across 4 emotional states:

1. **Happy (`happy`):**
   - Wide, sparkling anime-style eyes with glossy white highlights.
   - Open joyful crescent smile showing rosy cheeks.
   - Welcoming posture (waving hand/flipper/wing).
2. **Thinking (`thinking`):**
   - Tilted head angle (6° to 15°).
   - Eyes glancing upward with one raised eyebrow.
   - Hand/flipper brought to chin or glasses, indicating deep concentration.
3. **Cheering (`cheering`):**
   - Hands/wings thrown high into the air.
   - Laughing eyes (arched happy curves) or golden star sparkles.
   - Open triumphant celebration mouth; floating celebratory stars/confetti.
4. **Oops (`oops`):**
   - Gentle, supportive, and bashful (never frightening or shameful).
   - Head slightly tilted or tucked, sheepish smile, small sweatdrop or cute bandage.
   - Communicates: *"Oops, that's okay! Let's try again!"*

### React Native Animatable Shapes:
Because the vector silhouettes are constructed from simple geometric forms (circles, smooth beziers, rounded polygons), they can easily be animated using `react-native-reanimated` without heavy Lottie dependencies:
- **Wave:** Micro-rotation (`-10°` to `+14°`) around bottom origin.
- **Jump:** `translateY(-24px)` with squash-and-stretch scale (`1.08, 0.94`).
- **Slump:** Gentle soft compression (`scale(0.95, 0.92)`) for gentle supportive recovery.
- **Dance:** Alternating tilt and hop for completing full units.

## 5. Indian Children's Avatar Builder Architecture

The avatar builder is designed so that every child across India can look into the app and see themselves represented with pride.

### 1. Representation Options:
- **Skin Tones:** 4 authentic warm tones:
  - *Wheatish (गेहूंआ)* — Warm natural tone
  - *Golden Honey (स्वर्ण)* — Radiant warm olive
  - *Warm Caramel (सांवला)* — Rich warm hazelnut
  - *Deep Bronze* — Deep earthy brown
- **Hairstyles:**
  - Double plaits / braids tied with colourful ribbons
  - Neat side-part school crop
  - Curly bouncy mop
  - Sikh patka / topknot
  - Short wavy locks
- **Outfits:**
  - Classic Indian school uniform with striped tie
  - Festive colourful kurta / kurti (bright marigold & turquoise)
  - Everyday cosy hoodie with golden Chamku star badge
  - Junior Science lab coat with pocket pens
- **Accessories:**
  - Round spectacles, colourful sunglasses, sporty cricket cap, star hairpins.

### 2. Reward Mechanics (Mission-First Integrity):
- **100% Free & Practice-Earned:** No microtransactions, no real money, no paywalls.
- **Treasure Chest Unlocks:** Children open the daily evening treasure chest after finishing their 10 minutes of practice, unlocking stickers, new avatar accessories, or buddy outfits.
- **Weekly Streak Saver:** Unlocked purely by consistent practice, protecting their streak without anxiety.
