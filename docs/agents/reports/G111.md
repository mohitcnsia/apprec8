### Story 1: *The Council of the Forest* (`wp-bank-character-action`)
* **Length:** 127 words (Target: 120–180 words)  
* **Target words used (12 words):** *allay, pompous, incite, dauntless, virtuous, benevolent, philanthropist, alleviate, thwart, naive, coax, righteous*  
> *The wise old owl called an urgent assembly of forest elders to allay growing fears about the approaching timber company. A pompous badger began to complain loudly, trying to incite the gathered animals into a reckless and violent stampede. However, the dauntless white eagle intervened with steady authority, urging the woodland residents to remain virtuous and calm. He explained that a benevolent local philanthropist had purchased the entire valley to protect wildlife forever. This welcome news helped to alleviate the community's distress and thwart the timber merchants' destructive schemes. The animals were no longer naive enough to trust flattering promises from logging agents who had previously tried to coax them from their territory. Through unity and righteous action, the woodland sanctuary was permanently preserved for future generations.*

* **Length:** 123 words (Target: 120–180 words)  
* **Target words used (11 words):** *diligently, magnificent, meticulous, dawdle, bizarre, sedulous, frivolous, sophisticated, thrive, marvellous, lavish*  
> *Inside the quiet monastery sanctuary, an expert team worked diligently to restore a magnificent ceiling mural painted centuries ago. The head restorer was meticulous in cleaning every square inch of crumbling plaster, refusing to rush or dawdle over difficult sections. Some portions had suffered grotesque decay that looked almost bizarre to casual visitors, but her sedulous patience slowly revealed original vibrant pigments underneath. Rather than wasting time on frivolous discussions, the restorers used sophisticated microscopes and natural organic solvents to preserve delicate brush strokes. They watched the historic figures thrive once more under gentle museum lighting, emerging from dark shadows into marvellous clarity. When the scaffolding came down, the ancient temple looked lavish and regal, earning widespread acclaim from historians across the continent.*

### Story 3: *The Midnight Alarm* (`wp-bank-actions-change`)
