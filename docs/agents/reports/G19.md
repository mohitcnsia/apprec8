## 3. The 5 Least-Sure Questions (and Verification Details)

1. **`sq-bones-warmup-q1` (Baby vs Adult Bone Count):**
   - *Question:* "How many bones does a normal adult human skeleton contain?" (206 bones; baby born with ~300).
   - *Why unsure:* Medical literature cites ~270 to 300 bones at birth because ossification timing varies.
   - *Resolution:* Checked standard NCERT Class 5 / ICSE curricula and NSO past papers: "around 300 bones in a newborn which fuse into 206 adult bones" is the universally expected answer key.

2. **`sq-bones-gate-q2` (Floating Ribs Attachment):**
   - *Question:* "Why are the lowest two pairs of ribs in the rib cage called 'floating ribs'?" (Attached only to the spine; not joined to breastbone).
   - *Why unsure:* Kids might think "floating" means detached from all bones.
   - *Resolution:* Clarified in both the study page and question explanation that pairs 11 and 12 are firmly joined to the vertebral column in the back, but "float" free of the sternum in the front.

3. **`sq-food-gate-q1` (Small Intestine Villi vs Large Intestine):**
   - *Question:* "What are the tiny finger-like projections on the inner wall of the small intestine that absorb nutrients?" (Villi).
   - *Why unsure:* Does the large intestine also have villi?
   - *Resolution:* Anatomically, large intestine has intestinal crypts and microvilli, but does *not* possess prominent finger-like villi (which are specific to nutrient absorption in the duodenum, jejunum, and ileum). At Class 5 level, "villi = small intestine nutrient absorption" is standard.

4. **`sq-nervous-gate-q4` (Reflex Arc Pathway):**
   - *Question:* "A chef accidentally touches a boiling metal pot. Without thinking, their hand jerks backwards instantly. What pathway did this nerve impulse follow?"
   - *Why unsure:* Does the sensory signal reach the brain simultaneously?
   - *Resolution:* Yes, an impulse ascends via interneurons to the brain, but the motor command executing the withdrawal is initiated directly by the spinal cord reflex arc *before* the conscious sensation reaches the cerebrum. The question and explanation accurately highlight this survival mechanism.

5. **`sq-u2-test-q8` (Food Nutrient Chemical Tests):**
   - *Question:* Laboratory testing of food samples (Iodine test -> blue-black indicates starch).
   - *Why unsure:* Are chemical reagents appropriate for Class 5?
   - *Resolution:* NSO Class 5 papers frequently include experimental questions on food testing (dilute iodine for starch, copper sulphate + caustic soda for protein, paper smear for fat). This provides authentic Olympiad calibration without college jargon.
