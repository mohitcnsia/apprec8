## 3. Disputed Facts & Sourced Ranges Summary

1. **Domestication Timeline:**
   - Divergence estimates vary widely between paleogenomic molecular clocks (divergence 20,000–40,000+ years ago) and undisputed archaeological fossil records (14,000–16,000 years ago). Marked as `[DISPUTED]` with both ranges acknowledged.
2. **Olfactory Receptor Numbers:**
   - Human: 5–6 million.
   - Canine estimates range from 125 million in brachycephalic (short-nosed) breeds to over 300 million in Bloodhounds. German Shepherds represent the mesocephalic benchmark at ~225 million. Marked as `[VARIED ESTIMATE]`.
3. **Auditory Upper Limit:**
   - Upper hearing limit varies by sound pressure / decibel level in laboratory audiograms (45 kHz at 60 dB SPL up to 65 kHz at higher intensities). Marked as `[VARIED ESTIMATE]`.
4. **Bite Wound Care Protocol:**
   - Verified verbatim against **WHO Rabies Fact Sheet (2024)** and **NCDC India National Guidelines for Rabies Prophylaxis**. The 15-minute soap-and-running-water step and same-day anti-rabies vaccination requirement are highlighted with scientific justification (viral lipid dissolution).
