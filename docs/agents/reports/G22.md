4. **`eq-u4-spell-gate-q13` (Spelling of 'twelfth'):**
   - *Question:* Correct spelling of the ordinal 12th ('twelfth').
   - *Why unsure:* Children frequently spell it 'twelveth' by analogy with 'twelve'.
   - *Resolution:* The root 'twelve' drops '-ve' and replaces it with 'f' before '-th' ('twelfth'). Tested repeatedly in Indian primary spell bee competitions.
