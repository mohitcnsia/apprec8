All cards strictly follow project guidelines:
- **Card Count:** 4–7 cards per page.
- **Idea Cards:** Emoji, title, short text (≤ 40 words), ≤ 4 examples.
- **Check Cards:** ≥ 1 check card per page with 1 correct answer and 2–3 plausible wrong options (answer never in wrong list).
- **Tone & Style:** Kid-friendly, British/Indian English (`practise`, `colour`, `centre`).
