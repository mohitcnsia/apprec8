## 2. Fact Sources & References

All newly introduced facts are verifiable and checkable:

1. **Digital Payments Safety (UPI PIN):**
   - *Source:* National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) official consumer advisories (`npci.org.in`) and Reserve Bank of India (RBI) "RBI Kehta Hai" cyber awareness campaign.
   - *Rule:* UPI PIN is only needed to debit an account, never to receive money. Collect Requests must never be approved from unknown senders.

2. **Panchatantra (Clever Rabbit & Lion):**
   - *Source:* Pandit Vishnu Sharma, *Panchatantra*, Tantra 1 (Mitra-bheda), tale of *Shashaka-Simha Katha* ("बुद्धिर्यस्य बलं तस्य निर्बुद्धेश्च कुतो बलम्").
   - *Fact:* Classical Sanskrit fable teaching wisdom and patience over blind physical strength.

3. **Festivals of India:**
   - *Sources:* 
     - Deepavali: Ministry of Culture, Government of India (`indiaculture.gov.in`); victory of light over darkness and return of Shri Ram to Ayodhya.
     - Eid-ul-Fitr: Zakat and Fitra traditions of charity, gratitude, and communal brotherhood at the culmination of Ramadan.
     - Christmas: Universal commemoration of the birth of Jesus Christ, emphasizing peace and goodwill.
     - Pongal & Makar Sankranti: Agricultural harvest celebrations honoring Surya and nature's bounty.

4. **Rani Gaidinliu (1915–1993):**
   - *Sources:* Ministry of Culture, Government of India ("Azadi Ka Amrit Mahotsav" freedom fighter archives); National Informatics Centre (NIC) Manipur state portal.
   - *Facts:* Born in Tamenglong, Manipur; joined Jadonang's Heraka movement at age 13 to oppose British colonial taxation; captured in 1932 at age 16 and sentenced to life imprisonment; released in 1947 after Indian independence; met by Pt. Jawaharlal Nehru in Shillong jail in 1937 who described her as "Rani of the Nagas".
