     - **Source:** Kenneth W. Regan's Intrinsic Performance Rating models and empirical Lichess/Stockfish centipawn-loss regression distributions.
     - **Calibration Points:**
       - $\text{ACPL} \le 10 \text{ cp} \implies 2850 \text{ Elo}$ (Super Grandmaster)
       - $\text{ACPL} \approx 20 \text{ cp} \implies 2700 \text{ Elo}$ (Grandmaster)
       - $\text{ACPL} \approx 50 \text{ cp} \implies 2250 \text{ Elo}$ (Master / Expert)
       - $\text{ACPL} \approx 93.3 \text{ cp} \implies 1600 \text{ Elo}$ (Club / Intermediate)
       - $\text{ACPL} \ge 160 \text{ cp} \implies 600 \text{ Elo}$ (Casual floor)
   - Covered formula and multi-game rating logic in `tests/chessGame.test.mjs`.
