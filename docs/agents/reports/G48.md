## The 3 Directions (Meaning & Rationale)

### 1. `cosmic-star` ("Chamak & The Infinite Cosmos")
- **Concept:** Learning is an infinite celestial adventure through knowledge, wonder, and curiosity.
- **Infinity Motif:** A luminous golden stardust lemniscate whose right loop dissolves into ascending constellation pixels and star sparkles drifting into deep cosmic plum space.
- **Mascot Placement:** Chamak flies joyfully at the apex of the ribbon, wearing a warm confident smile and blushing cheeks, guiding children across the educational cosmos.

### 2. `infinite-bloom` ("Growth, Sprout & Wonder")
- **Concept:** Learning as organic personal vitality—a calm, encouraging environment where children blossom naturally every single day.
- **Infinity Motif:** A living botanical vine in gentle lilac and mauve with golden leaf sprouts (strictly adhering to no green checks), whose right loop breaks into floating digital pollen cubes and dandelion seeds.
- **Mascot Placement:** Chamak sits as the radiant golden flower blossom at the living heart of the loop, expressing warmth, nurture, and well-being.

### 3. `playful-geometric` ("Modern Joy & Digital Discovery")
- **Concept:** Crisp, contemporary educational badge geometry tailored for modern students, teachers, and schools.
- **Infinity Motif:** A bold dual-tone vector ribbon (Star Gold face, Lilac inner stripe, Deep Plum shadow) whose right loop deconstructs cleanly into precision isometric voxel/pixel cubes.
- **Mascot Placement:** Chamak serves as an embossed modern geometric star badge crest, lending high-contrast legibility and brand authority across digital platforms.
