## 1. Executive Summary

Task G113 implements linking rewards (+100 ⭐ bonus granted once per child when parent–child link is established) along with server-side security safeguards and user-facing motivational nudges, strictly following doc `https://claude.ai/artifact/C3dWqo9YLhc5HEHACzb9vD` (section 4):

1. **Server-Side Security (`familyActions.js`):**
   - Caller identity check: `createChildProfile` and `createParentLinkCode` verify the caller is not a student account (`if (Number.isInteger(callerData.grade) && !(callerData.childUids || []).length) throw new HttpsError("permission-denied", ...)`).
2. **Server-Side Bonus Grant (`familyActions.js` & `joinActions.js`):**
   - `createChildProfile`: Initializes child with `stats: { totalStars: 100, currentStreak: 0 }` and `linkBonusAt: now`.
   - `redeemParentLinkCode`: Checks `!kidData.linkBonusAt` inside the transaction. If not previously awarded, atomically writes `linkBonusAt: FieldValue.serverTimestamp()` and updates `"stats.totalStars": FieldValue.increment(100)`. Subsequent parent links (or re-linking) skip the bonus.
   - `joinClassByLink`: Grants +100 stars and sets `linkBonusAt` when a parent establishes the link during class join consent.
   - The phone NEVER awards or increments stars directly.
3. **App Experience:**
   - **Leaderboard (`screens/progress/LeaderboardScreen.js`):** Unlinked students see a friendly, non-punitive lock screen:
     - Title: *"Link with your parent to join the leaderboard"*
     - Subtext: *"Connect with your parent to see where you stand with classmates, cheer on friends, and earn +100 ⭐ bonus stars!"*
     - Button: *"Link with parent · +100 ⭐"* navigating directly to `Profile` -> `LinkParent` (`initial: false`).
     - Halts network fetching for unlinked learners.
   - **Me Tab (`screens/profile/MeScreen.js`):** Unlinked learners see a prominent, friendly card:
     - Title: *"Link with your parent: +100 ⭐"*
     - Subtext: *"Connect with your parent to unlock the leaderboard and earn 100 bonus stars!"*
     - Tapping navigates directly to `LinkParent`.
   - **Zero Learning Gates:** Quest tree, practice, and lessons remain completely ungated and open.
