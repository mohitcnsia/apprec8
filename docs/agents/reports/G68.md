## 1. Investigation Findings: Where G28's 150 Questions Went & Why Class 5 Couldn't Reach Them

- `wp-antonyms-mix-1` (22 Qs) & `wp-antonyms-mix-2` (25 Qs) → attached to `synAntP3` (`id: "wp-synant-p3"`, Title: `"Opposites Mix"`, subtopic: `"antonyms"`, branchId: `"wp-synant"`).
- `wp-vocab-mix` (25 Qs) → attached to `collocationsP2` (`id: "wp-colloc-p2"`, Title: `"Word Mix"`, subtopic: `"idiomatic-collocations"`, branchId: `"wp-colloc"`).
- `wp-idioms-mix` (18 Qs) → attached to `idiomsP2` (`id: "wp-idioms-p2"`, Title: `"Idiom Mix"`, subtopic: `"action-phrases"`, branchId: `"wp-idioms"` in Unit 2).
*(Total: 136 kept, 14 duplicate concepts dropped).*

### 1.2 Why a Strong Class 5 Reader (Pratha) Bounced Off
1. **Trapped by Spec S Starter Ladder (`wp-sameopp`):**
   In `data/wordPowerQuestData.js` (line 4447), `SAMEOPP_GRADES = [2, 3, 4, 5]`. This inserted `sameOpp` ("Same & Opposite") at `branchOrder: -1` ahead of the rest of the quest. When a Class 5 child opens Word Power, the app greets them with toddler-level pairs:
   - *"Which word means the same as BIG? large"*
   - *"Which word means the same as HAPPY? glad"*
   - *"Which word is the opposite of HOT? cold"*
   - *"Which word is the opposite of UP? down"*
   - *"Which word is the opposite of DAY? night"*
   - *"Which word is the opposite of PUSH? pull"*
2. **Hard Sequential Main Node Lock:**
   In `utils/questTree.js` (`buildQuestPath`), progression along the main quest spine is strictly sequential (`previousBranchCleared`). Because `wp-sameopp` sits at `branchOrder: -1`, `wp-synant` (at `branchOrder: 0`) is **hard-locked** (`status: "locked"`) until the child completes both the `wp-sameopp-study` card and the `wp-sameopp-warmup` quiz.
3. **Practice Leaves Buried on Side Rails:**
   Even when `wp-synant` is unlocked, G28's mixes sit on **side-branch practice leaves** (`synAntP2` and `synAntP3`), which on the Quest Map are rendered off the main path or folded into a summary chip (`⭐ 0/3 practice ›`). If a child follows the golden path (Main → Challenge Gate → Next Topic), they never encounter them.
4. **Collocations & Idioms Gated Further Down:**
   `wp-vocab-mix` requires finishing `wp-colloc` (branch 2), and `wp-idioms-mix` is in Unit 2 (requiring clearing the Unit 1 Boss). A Class 5 child looking for rich synonym/antonym practice gave up long before seeing any of them.

> **Recommendation:** Update `SAMEOPP_GRADES = [2, 3, 4]` so Class 5 strong readers begin directly on `Synonyms and Antonyms` without being forced through the toddler warm-up.

## 2. The Champion Rung Implementation

To provide high-difficulty, engaging practice for strong readers, we added a **Champion** rung to Word Power under both **Synonyms & Antonyms** (Unit 1) and **Idioms** (Unit 2).

### 2.1 Node Configuration
1. **`wp-synant-champion`** (attached to `wp-synant` in Unit 1):
   - **Title:** `"Challenge: for word lovers"`
   - **Difficulty:** `"hard"` (renders with `#FF4B4B` challenge red border/icon on Quest Map)
   - **Grade Tag:** Untagged (`grades: undefined`), meaning **open to all classes** (no grade filter).
   - **Subtopic:** `"synonyms"` (eligible for "My mistakes" practice loops).
   - **Steps:** 3 quizzes (70 questions total):
     - `wp-synant-champion-1` (25 questions: Advanced Synonyms)
     - `wp-synant-champion-2` (25 questions: Advanced Antonyms)
     - `wp-synant-champion-3` (20 questions: Curated Master Challenge Words)
   - **Unlock Rule:** Unlocked immediately once the parent topic (`wp-synant`) is completed, or **straight away** in tester mode (`unlockAll: true`).

2. **`wp-idioms-champion`** (attached to `wp-idioms` in Unit 2):
   - **Title:** `"Challenge: for word lovers"`
   - **Difficulty:** `"hard"`
   - **Grade Tag:** Untagged (open to all classes).
   - **Subtopic:** `"action-phrases"`.
   - **Steps:** 1 quiz (20 questions total):
     - `wp-idioms-champion-1` (20 questions: Nuanced Idioms & Figurative Expressions)
   - **Unlock Rule:** Unlocked once `wp-idioms` is completed, or straight away in tester mode.
