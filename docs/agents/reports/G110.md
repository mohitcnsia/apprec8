Task G110 addresses the review findings from G109 to ensure the chess library in Firestore is authentic, strictly validated against opening rules, free of duplicates, and fully equipped with Stockfish engine evaluations (`evals > 0` for all 25 games):

1. **Engine Verdicts (`evals`):** Merged Stockfish evaluations into all 25 games. All 25 docs now have `evals > 0` (range: 9 to 67 evaluated positions per game). The app will now display the "engine ✓" badge and colored guess verdicts.
2. **Duplicate Removed:** Deleted duplicate doc `01-morphy-paul-duke-karl-count-isouard-1858` from Firestore. `opera-game` is maintained as the single canonical ID.
3. **Misfiled Openings Replaced:**
   - **London System:** Replaced non-London games with genuine London games from `London2e6.pgn`:
     - `01-kamsky-g-del-rio-de-angelis-s-2016` (Kamsky vs Del Rio, Sunway Sitges 2016, ECO A40, White wins 1-0).
     - `01-kamsky-g-taborsky-r-2015` (Kamsky vs Taborsky, 2015 Lasker Mem, ECO A46, White wins 1-0).
   - **Sicilian Accelerated Dragon:** Replaced Classical Sicilian (2...d6) games with genuine Accelerated Dragon games (1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 + ...g6, no early ...d6, Black wins 0-1) from `SicilianAccelDragon.pgn`:
     - `01-short-nigel-d-larsen-bent-1985` (Nigel Short vs Bent Larsen, 1985 Naestved, ECO B38, Black wins 0-1 in 23 moves).
     - `01-azarov-s-bogdanovich-s-2017` (Sergei Azarov vs Stanislav Bogdanovich, 2017 Lviv Rapid Cup, ECO B34, Black wins 0-1 in 19 moves).
4. **Programmatic Opening Validator (`validate_opening`):** Added strict check in `scripts/chess/extract_and_build_library.py` enforcing:
   - London System: `d4` + `Bf4` in first 5 White moves, no `c4` in first 5 moves.
   - King's Gambit: `1.e4 e5 2.f4`.
   - Sicilian Accelerated Dragon: `1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6` + `...g6`, no `...d6` in Black's first 5 moves.
   - Bogo-Indian: `d4`, `c4` in first 3 White moves; `Nf6`, `e6`, `Bb4(+)` in first 3 Black moves.
5. **Re-seed Never Erases Annotations:** Implemented `mergePreservingAnnotations` in `scripts/chess/seedChessGame.mjs`. When re-seeding runs, any existing move comments, `commentBefore`, `trivia`, custom user fields, and top-level notes/trivia are strictly preserved.
6. **Device Verification Rule:** Applied board rule: *"phone only, never the emulator. Plug-in requests go in your report."* Emulator killed; plug-in verification request detailed below.
