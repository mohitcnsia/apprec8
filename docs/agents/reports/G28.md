## 3. Fixed Questions (Flawed Keys, Spellings, and Options)

The legacy JSON contained several flawed keys, non-British spellings, and broken options that failed unit tests:

1. **`4ieo11-synonyms-q1` Q4 (`Diminish`):**  
   *Old key:* `Belittle` (flawed — belittle means to dismiss as unimportant).  
   *Fixed key:* `lessen` ("To diminish means to make or become less; lessen is its direct synonym").
2. **`4ieo11-synonyms-q1` Q7 (`Strive`):**  
   *Old key:* `Aim` (inaccurate — aim is merely to intend or point).  
   *Fixed key:* `struggle` ("To strive means to make great efforts or struggle energetically to achieve something").
3. **`4ieo11-synonyms-q1` Q11 (`Certainty`):**  
   *Old key:* `Surety` (archaic / financial guarantee / bail).  
   *Fixed key:* `conviction` ("Certainty means firm conviction that something is the case").
4. **`4ieo11-synonyms-q1` Q12 (`Obedient`):**  
   *Old key:* `Devoted` (confused — devoted means loving and loyal).  
   *Fixed key:* `dutiful` ("Obedient means complying with orders or rules; dutiful is its proper synonym").
5. **`4ieo11-synonyms-q1` Q13 (`Coax`):**  
   *Old key:* `Tempt` (enticement trap).  
   *Fixed key:* `persuade` ("To coax means to persuade someone gently and gradually").
6. **`4ieo11-synonyms-q1` Q19 (`Repulsive`):**  
   *Old key:* `Hateful` (inaccurate emotion).  
   *Fixed key:* `revolting` ("Repulsive means arousing intense distaste or disgust; revolting is its synonym").
7. **`4ieo11-synonyms-q2` Q22 (`Energy`):**  
   *Old spelling:* `Vigor` (US spelling).  
   *Fixed spelling:* `vigour` (British English with 'our').
8. **`4ieo11-antonyms-q1` Q2 (`Endure`):**  
   *Old key:* `Reject` (inaccurate).  
   *Fixed key:* `surrender` ("To endure means to remain firm under hardship; to surrender or give up is its antonym").
9. **`4ieo11-antonyms-q1` Q9 (`Mingle`):**  
   *Old key:* `Disjoin` (awkward archaic word).  
   *Fixed key:* `isolate` ("To mingle means to associate with others; to isolate or separate is its opposite").
10. **`4ieo11-antonyms-q1` Q14 (`Resent`):**  
    *Old key:* `Love` (imprecise).  
    *Fixed key:* `welcome` ("To resent means to feel bitterness at something; to welcome or appreciate is its opposite").
11. **`4ieo11-vocab-q1` Q4 (`meet / meat`):**  
    *Old options:* `["meat / meet", "meet / meat", "meat / meat", "meet / meet"]` (3 identical duplicate options — crashed `assert.equal(optSet.size, 4)`).  
    *Fixed options:* `["meet / meat", "meat / meet", "meet / mete", "mete / meat"]` (4 distinct options with specific homophone mistake tags).
