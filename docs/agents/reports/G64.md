# G64 Report: Parent's Week Card & Progress Refresh

**Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Branch:** `gemini/g64-parent-week`  
**Owner Problem Addressed:**
> "I can't see Pratha's progress for today. I had to select Science because she only did Science; with 10 subjects I'd click every subject. Not intuitive."

---

## 1. What was Broken (Root Cause)

1. **One-Time Load on Mount:** `ParentWeekCard.js` had a single `useEffect` dependent on `[child.uid, child.grade]`. If the parent kept the Progress screen open, or navigated between tabs, it never fetched updated data from Firestore.
2. **Subject Splitting & Non-Intuitive Navigation:** Parents previously saw individual subject entries or had to hunt across subjects. If a child only did one subject (like Science), the summary wasn't immediately apparent.
3. **No Pull-to-Refresh:** `ProgressScreen` had no `RefreshControl` on the parent (`view === "family"`) or teacher (`view === "classes"`) scroll views.
4. **Teacher View Stale on Focus:** `TeacherClasses.js` also only fetched classes once on initial mount without re-querying when navigating back to the tab.

---

## 2. Changes Made

### A. All Subjects Combined & Intuitive Layout (`utils/parentWeek.js`)
- **Combined Header Format:**
  - `timeText` now formats as:
    `"This week: <N> days · <total_time> · <Subject 1> <time 1>, <Subject 2> <time 2>"`
    *(e.g., `"This week: 6 days · 2 h 21 min · English Quest 1 h 18 min, Science Quest 1 h 3 min"`)*
  - Hindi translation supported:
    `"इस हफ़्ते: 6 दिन · 2 घंटे 21 मिनट · English Quest 1 घंटा 18 मिनट, Science Quest 1 घंटा 3 मिनट"`
- **Most Recent First & Expanded:**
  - Subjects with practice this week are sorted by `lastActiveAt` descending (latest activity first).
  - All practised subjects render directly in the card without requiring the parent to click a subject selector.
- **Folding Unpractised Subjects:**
  - Subjects with 0 activity this week are folded into a single clean line:
    `"Not practised this week: <Subject A>, <Subject B>"`
    *(Hindi: `"इस हफ़्ते अभ्यास नहीं किया: <Subject A>, <Subject B>"`)*

### B. Auto-Refresh on Focus & Pull-to-Refresh (`ProgressScreen.js`, `ParentWeekCard.js`)
- **Focus Reload:**
  - `ParentWeekCard.js` now uses `useFocusEffect(load)` and also listens to `refreshTrigger`.
  - `ProgressScreen.js` uses `useFocusEffect` to increment `refreshKey` and reload the child list.
- **Pull-to-Refresh:**
  - Wired `RefreshControl` to `ScrollView` in `view === "family"`. Pulling down triggers an immediate re-fetch of `myChildren` and re-renders `ParentWeekCard` with fresh data.

### C. Teacher Class Refresh (`TeacherClasses.js`)
- Added `useFocusEffect` to re-fetch `myTeachingClasses` and increment `reloadKey` on tab focus.
- Wired `refreshTrigger` prop so pull-to-refresh on `view === "classes"` refreshes teacher class rosters and student progress.

### D. Unit Tests (`tests/parentWeek.test.mjs`)
- Added test coverage for:
  - Combined `timeText` with per-subject breakdown.
  - Sorting practised subjects with most recent first.
  - Folding unpractised subjects into `unpractisedText`.
  - Hindi localisations for combined header and unpractised string.
  - All test assertions passing.

## 3. Investigation: Practice Paper Storage

As requested by the owner, we inspected `screens/paper/` and `services/paperApi.js`:
- **Current State:**
  - Practice papers save session history to local device storage via `AsyncStorage` (`paperHistory`).
  - Completed papers record a single attempt to the `questAttempts` Firestore collection.
  - Mistake tags from papers are written to `users/{userId}/questProgress/{questId}.mistakes`.
  - **Crucially:** Practice papers do **not** maintain a `questProgress/practice-papers.stats` document.
- **Firestore Permission Boundary:**
  - Per Firestore security rules (`DR-10`), a parent can only read `users/{childUid}/questProgress/*`.
  - `questAttempts` is restricted to `auth.uid == resource.data.userId` (the child's own phone).
- **Implication for Parent View:**
  - Because practice papers do not update `questProgress/{questId}.stats.daily`, practice paper time and question counts are currently **not** aggregated into the parent's weekly card.
  - **Recommended Next Step for Papers:** When recording a paper attempt, write the time and question count to `users/{userId}/questProgress/practice-papers` (or the respective subject's `questProgress.stats.daily`), so parents can see paper practice alongside quest practice.
