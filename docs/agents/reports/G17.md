## 2. Topic Details

### Topic 0: One or Many? (`eq-u3-basics`)
- **Study Page:** `eq-u3-basics` ("Subject and Verb: Best Friends"). Covers singular subjects taking verb with -s/-es, plural subjects taking base verbs without -s, special rules for "I" and "You", and common helping verbs (is/are, was/were, has/have).
- **Subtopics for Gate:**
  1. `singular-subject` (5 questions in pool, 3 in main warm-up)
  2. `plural-subject` (5 questions in pool, 3 in main warm-up)
  3. `pronouns-i-you` (5 questions in pool, 2 in main warm-up, 6 in practice leaf)
- **Practice Leaf:** `eq-u3-basics-p1` ("The 'I' and 'You' Rules", easy).

### Topic 1: Tricky Subjects (`eq-u3-tricky`)
- **Study Page:** `eq-u3-tricky` ("Tricky Subjects: Each, Every and Pairs"). Covers indefinite pronouns (*each*, *every*, *everyone*, *somebody* taking singular verbs), subjects joined by *and* (plural) vs *or* / *nor* (agreement with closest subject), and *a pair of* vs standalone plural nouns (*scissors*, *shoes*, *trousers*).
- **Subtopics for Gate:**
  1. `each-everyone` (5 questions in pool, 3 in main warm-up, 6 in practice leaf 1)
  2. `compound-subjects` (5 questions in pool, 3 in main warm-up)
  3. `pair-collective` (5 questions in pool, 2 in main warm-up, 6 in practice leaf 2)
- **Practice Leaves:**
  - `eq-u3-tricky-p1` ("Each, Every & Everyone", medium)
  - `eq-u3-tricky-p2` ("A Pair of...", hard)

### Topic 2: There is & There are (`eq-u3-there`)
- **Study Page:** `eq-u3-there` ("There is, There are and Questions"). Covers inverted word order in *There is / There are* and *Here is / Here are* (locating the real subject behind the verb), uncountable nouns with *There is*, and asking questions with *Do* vs *Does* (reverting main verb to base form after *Does*).
- **Subtopics for Gate:**
  1. `there-is-are` (5 questions in pool, 3 in main warm-up)
  2. `here-is-are` (5 questions in pool, 2 in main warm-up)
  3. `questions-do-does` (5 questions in pool, 3 in main warm-up, 6 in practice leaf)
- **Practice Leaf:** `eq-u3-there-p1` ("Do or Does?", medium).
