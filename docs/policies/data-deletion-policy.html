Delete Your Apprec8 Account and Data

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

This page explains how to delete an account in Apprec8 (the learning app run by Mohit Kumar Chilkoti, India) and what happens to the data. It goes with our Privacy Policy.

1. Delete your account in the App (fastest)

  1. Open Apprec8 and sign in.
  2. Tap the Me tab, scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  3. Type the confirmation phrase and tap Confirm & Delete Account.

The account and its data are deleted straight away.

2. Delete a child's profile (parents)

A child profile you created from your account: Me → My children → tap the child → Delete profile. A child who has their own login can delete their account from their own Me tab, or you can ask us by email.

3. Without the App

Email mohitchilkoti@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from the email address of the account (a parent may write for their child). We may reply to confirm it is your account. We complete the deletion within 7 days.

4. What is deleted

  • The sign-in account (email, name, profile photo link).
  • The profile: first name, class, avatar.
  • All learning data: answers, scores, time spent, stars, streaks, progress and mistakes.
  • Class memberships and, for teachers, their place in their classes.
  • Parent and child links and the parent PIN; unused login and link codes.
  • For a parent: the child profiles they created (children with their own login keep their account).
  • Weekly summary notification settings.

5. What is kept, and why

  • A record that parental consent was given (type, version and date, with the parent's name removed), because the law may require us to show consent was given.
  • The text of question reports (for example "the answer to question 3 looks wrong"), with the name and account removed, so we can fix the question for other children.

Nothing else is kept. We have no ads and never sold or shared the data.

6. Contact

Questions about deletion: mohitchilkoti@gmail.com (Mohit Kumar Chilkoti, Grievance Officer).
