Privacy Policy for Apprec8

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

In short:

  • Apprec8 is a learning app for school children, their parents and their teachers.
  • We collect only what the app needs to teach and to show progress to the child, their parent and their teacher.
  • No ads. We never sell data or use it for advertising. We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • Children can't chat or send messages to other users.
  • You can delete your account, and everything in it, from inside the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Apprec8 ("we", "us") is run by Mohit Kumar Chilkoti, India. This policy explains what the Apprec8 mobile app (the "App") collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. It follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and Google Play's Families policy.

3. How we use it

  • To run the App: lessons, quizzes, stars, progress and practice of past mistakes.
  • To show progress to the people the child is connected to (see section 4).
  • To send a weekly summary to parents and teachers who turned it on (children never get notifications).
  • To fix and improve our questions and lessons, using answers and question reports.
  • To keep the App safe and working (for example, stopping misuse of class codes).

We never use personal data for advertising, never sell it, and never build profiles to target children.

4. Who can see what

  • Other students: on leaderboards, only your first name, class and stars. Never your last name, email or school.
  • Your teacher: only after the teacher approves you into their class: your first name, class, progress, scores, time spent and topics you find hard in that class.
  • Your parent: only a parent linked to your account: practice days, time, scores, strong and weak topics, and recent mistakes.
  • The Apprec8 team: to fix questions and keep the App working.

5. Children

Apprec8 is made for children, including children under 13. Under the DPDP Act, a child is anyone under 18, so we ask for a parent's consent:

  • A parent can create a child profile from their own account, after giving consent.
  • To join a class, the parent's name and relation are recorded as consent, and the teacher (who knows the family) approves the child.
  • Linking a child's own account to a parent needs a code from the parent, with the parent's consent.

Children cannot chat with or message other users, see ads or make purchases. The "Need help?" screen shows national helplines (1098, 14416, 112); a call is made through the phone's own dialer and we don't record it.

If you think a child is using Apprec8 without a parent's knowledge, write to us (section 11) and we will help or delete the account.

6. Service providers

We don't share personal data with third parties. These services process it for us, only to run the App:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database, server functions, app settings, notifications). Data is stored on Google Cloud servers, which may be outside India.
  • Expo (delivers app updates; receives technical information such as the app version and device type).
  • jsDelivr (delivers pictures used in lessons; like any website, it sees the phone's internet address).
  • Google Play (installing and updating the App).

We may disclose data only if Indian law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

As long as the account exists. When an account is deleted, its data is deleted as described in section 8.

8. Deleting your data

  • Your own account: in the App, Me → Delete my account.
  • A child profile you made as a parent: Me → My children → tap the child → Delete profile.
  • Without the App: email us (section 11) from the account's email address.

Deleted at once: the sign-in account, profile, progress, answers, stars, class memberships, parent links, parent PIN, and child profiles the parent made. Kept: a record that consent was given, with names removed (the law may ask us to show consent was given), and the text of question reports with the name removed (so we can fix the question). Full details: Data Deletion Policy.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to act for you. A parent can do this for their child. Most of it you can do in the App (Me → Edit profile, Delete my account); for the rest, write to us.

10. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules let each person see only what this policy allows. Parent PINs are stored only as scrambled codes. No system is perfectly safe; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Contact and grievances

Questions, requests or complaints (Grievance Officer): Mohit Kumar Chilkoti, email mohitchilkoti@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above and tell users in the App about important changes.
