**Full description** (no emojis: owner 2026-11-06; the parent-yes line returns when learn first ships):

Apprec8 (say it "appreciate") helps every child learn a little every day, at their own pace, with fun and care.

Learn smarter. Think sharper. Feel better.

English and Science today, with Maths and reasoning coming next.

LEARN BY DOING
• English Quest: grammar, words and reading, from first sentences to Class 10 grammar (tenses, modals, reported
  speech, active and passive voice, editing).
• Science Quest: plants, the human body, light, sound, magnets, puzzles and more.
• Short Learn cards before each topic, then practice that gets harder step by step.
• Every answer explains why it is right.

PRACTICE THAT STICKS
• "My mistakes" brings back the questions your child got wrong, until they get them right twice.
• Practice papers to try a full paper with a clock.
• Stars and streaks reward effort, not just marks.

FOR PARENTS
• A weekly summary: days practised, time spent, strong topics and one topic to practise.
• "One thing to do at home": a two-minute idea to help your child.
• English and हिंदी.
• Several children on one phone, each with their own profile, and a timer for play time.

FOR TEACHERS AND SCHOOLS
• Create a class and share a code; you approve every student.
• Assign topics and see who needs help, which topic to reteach, and who is ready for more.

SAFE FOR CHILDREN
• No ads. No chat. Children can't message anyone.
• Children see only first names and classes on leaderboards.
• "Need help?" shows national helplines (1098 and 14416).
• Delete an account and all its data from inside the app, any time.

Made in India by a parent, with a teacher, for children.
